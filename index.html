The Atelier

An idea that crossed the Alps.

The first sketch of DANILO wasn't drawn by a watchmaker — it came from a furniture designer's studio in Milan, who treated the case like a piece of architecture: proportion first, ornament last. That drawing travelled north to a small atelier outside Geneva, where it became a movement, a case, a bracelet — each part cut, brushed and assembled by hand. It's this collision of Italian form and Swiss precision that gives DANILO its quiet, unmistakable confidence.

Four Finishes

The Collection

Each DANILO begins from the same architecture — only the surface changes. Choose the one that matches how you move through a day.

Steel

Polished & Brushed
+ The Story

The original expression of the design, in 316L surgical steel. Polished chamfers alternate with brushed planes so the case reads differently in every light — nothing plated, nothing to wear through over time. This is the watch as it was first drawn.

Noir

Black PVD
+ The Story

A deep black PVD layer, fused directly to the steel beneath. Chosen for the hours a watch should be felt rather than seen — under a cuff, across a dinner table, on a night that doesn't need to announce itself.

Marine

Sunburst Blue
+ The Story

A sunburst blue mixed to sit somewhere between the Adriatic at dusk and dark raw denim — warm enough to feel personal, dark enough to stay quiet in a boardroom. Our most requested colorway before it existed.

Reserve

Rose Gold
+ The Story

An 18k rose-gold-tone case, warmer and quieter than yellow gold. Named Reserve because it's the finish we'd keep for ourselves — made for people who don't need the loudest option in the room to feel noticed.
